How it works

From a question to a decision, in five steps.

Jina connects institutions and citizens through one continuous loop — feedback in, insight out.

Ask

Institutions create short, focused surveys using ready workflows for healthcare, government, business and community needs.

Target

Define exactly who should respond — recent visitors, a location, an age group, or a time window — so feedback reflects real experience.

Answer

Citizens receive relevant surveys in a simple mobile experience and respond in about two minutes.

Reward

Every honest response earns Jina Points — a transparent way to recognize participation and build trust over time.

Analyze

Responses are processed in real time into live dashboards — completion, trust scores, and the issues that matter most.

Decide

Jina turns the top issue into a clear recommendation — so institutions know exactly what to fix first.
